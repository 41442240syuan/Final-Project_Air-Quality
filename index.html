Objectives¶

In this project, we would like to look at the air pollution and weather data. We want to find out the relationships between traffic emissions (like CO and NO2) and weather conditions.

Plot¶

Daily Hourly Trends of CO and NOx Concentrations¶

In [30]:
hourly_trend = df_clean.groupby('Hour')[['CO(GT)', 'NOx(GT)']].mean()
X = hourly_trend.index

# Plot 1: CO trend. Get CO data for Y axis
Y1 = hourly_trend['CO(GT)']
plt.plot(X, Y1)
plt.title("CO Concentration") 
plt.show()
# Plot 2: NOx trend. Get NOx data for Y axis
Y2 = hourly_trend['NOx(GT)']
plt.plot(X, Y2)
plt.title("NOx Concentration")
plt.show()

Observation and Conclusion¶

From the line charts, we can see the average CO and NOx concentrations in 24 hours. There are two highest points for both gases: one is around 8:00 AM, and the other is around 6:00 PM. Might because of rush hours. People drive cars to work and go home. This means cars cause the air pollution.

The scatter plot shows the relationship between CO and NO2. We can see that most dots go up to the top right. When CO goes up, NO2 also goes up. They probably come from the same pollution source
